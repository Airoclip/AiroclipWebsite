How does the free trial work?

Confirm the free 3-day Trial in the App Store to use Nazar, the water bottle with water reminders, and the photo locket with photo editing for 72 consecutive hours. The clock continues while Swaylet is closed. When the trial ends, desktop charms stop until you purchase Swaylet Lifetime.

Other charms, custom reminders, gifts, and GIF export require Swaylet Lifetime. It is a separate one-time purchase. The trial does not renew or charge you automatically.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Swaylet’s purchase window and select Restore purchase while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring a trial keeps its original start time. If access is still missing, email us.

Where is the app after I close its window?

Swaylet lives in your Mac’s menu bar. Click its eye icon to access the app’s controls. If a desktop charm is missing, check whether it is hidden or your trial has ended.

Need help with a photo locket or a reminder?

Email us with the steps you tried and what you expected to happen. Reminders run while Swaylet is open. You do not need to send your personal photos to ask for help.
